Astrophysics Briefing: The Transit Method

The Kepler Space Telescope monitors stellar brightness over time. When an exoplanet crosses directly between Earth and its host star, it creates a minuscule "shadow" that temporarily dips starlight intensity. By detecting these recurring periodic dips, astronomers confirm alien planetary systems without ever seeing them directly.
